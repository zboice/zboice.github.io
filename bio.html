I'm a fourth year undergraduate at SUNY Geneseo, studying Physics and Math. In my spare time, I am the president of Geneseo's Physics and Astronomy Club, I sing in an acapella group, and I play the cello. I also enjoy learning about random physics topics and then creating simulations or visualizations.

For the past three years, I've been doing research with Dr. Thomas Osburn on gravitational wave source modeling. Importantly, I implemented a hyperboloidal slicing and compactification scheme which improved the boundary conditions for a time-domain code with circular orbits. Some of our current work is on broadening this scheme to easily accomodate eccentric geodesics, and to eventually allow for inspirals. We are also developing a scheme to calculate gravitational self-force numerically, starting with a toy model based on electric charges.

I am also involved in an ongoing project with Gavin George (_) and Chris Desiderio (_) to address light pollution on the SUNY Geneseo campus. Our goal is to collect data about light levels on campus so we can educate the people who make decisions about campus lighting.
